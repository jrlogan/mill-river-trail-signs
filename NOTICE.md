# Rights

This repository holds three different kinds of material. They are **not** under
the same terms, and the MIT licence covers only the first.

## 1. The build system — MIT

Everything in `build/`, the workflow in `.github/`, and the structure of the
content files. Reuse it freely; that is why this repo is public. If you are
building interpretive signage somewhere else, the copy-fit and QR-verification
pieces are the parts worth stealing.

## 2. Sign text — © the project and its researchers

The English and Spanish copy in `content/*.yml` was researched and written for
the Mill River Trail, principally by **Colin M. Caplan**, with translation and
editing by the project. Not licensed for reuse. Ask.

## 3. Images — third party, individually credited

The historic photographs and drawings in `assets/images/` were supplied for this
signage project by their holders and are **not** covered by the MIT licence.
They are credited in the captions in `content/*.yml`, and include material
courtesy of:

- **New Haven Museum**
- **Submarine Force Museum**
- **Colin M. Caplan** (personal collection)
- **Joseph Taylor Collection**

If you want to use any of them, go to the holder, not to us.

### Species illustrations

`noun-black-capped-chickadee-color.svg` and `noun-yellow-rumped-warber-color.svg`
came from **The Noun Project**. Noun Project artwork is licensed either CC BY
(which *requires* visible attribution to the creator) or royalty-free
(which does not, but must be purchased). **Before these signs are fabricated,
confirm which licence applies** and, if it is CC BY, add the creator credit to
the sign's credit bar. `Sumac-color.svg` needs the same check.

### Map panels

Locator maps are generated from **OpenStreetMap**, © OpenStreetMap contributors,
licensed **ODbL**. The attribution printed in the corner of each map satisfies
that licence and must stay on the sign.
